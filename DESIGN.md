## Direction
- Keep the colors as defined above.
- Tint neutrals.
- Remove AI tells (icon tiles above headings, nested cards, gray text on image backgrounds).
- Replace the "hacker interface" density with calmer, more confident layouts (fewer elements per screen, larger type, more space).
- Everything reads as one calm operation: restraint over decoration.
- Motion personality: confident and quick. Entrances ease out, nothing bounces. Storytelling sections scroll-driven. Controls respond in about 180ms. Everything has a reduced-motion version.
- Structural ideas: dark-first editorial, mono eyebrows, one accent reserved for the top CTA (Sanity), cinematic dark heroes with clean reading bands (Runway), precision (Linear), enterprise AI (Cohere).
