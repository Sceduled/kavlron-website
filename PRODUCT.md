# PRODUCT

- **Product:** Kalvron AI OS, a done-for-you AI operating system that runs a company's departments as one system.
- **Surface:** marketing website for Kalvron (home page, partners page, legal pages).
- **Audience:** owners and operators of enterprises and SMEs in India with roughly 50 lakh to 10 crore rupees of monthly revenue, who want to grow without hiring to keep up.
- **Purpose of the site:** make a visitor understand in seconds what Kalvron delivers, trust it, and book a 20-minute call.
- **Visitor mode:** skeptical, time-poor business owner skimming on mobile and desktop.
- **Voice:** calm, confident, plain. No hype, no jargon. Short sentences.
- **Primary action:** Book a call.
- **Constraints:** keep the rolling video hero background; keep existing brand colors; no prices; no client stories or statistics; client logos only; literal security claims; no secrets on the client.
- **Evidence available:** client names and logos only.
- **Anti-goals:** generic SaaS look, card-heavy layouts, purple gradients, default fonts, bounce animations, anything that makes it look like another AI agency template.
- **Competitors studied for structure only:** Fulcrum Labs AI (fulcrumlabs.pro) and WeLaunch (welaunch.ai). Never imitate their look or copy their wording.
